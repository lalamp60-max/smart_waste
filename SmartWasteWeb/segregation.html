♻️ Waste Segregation Tips

🟢 Wet Waste

  • Food leftovers
  • Vegetable & fruit peels
  • Tea leaves, eggshells

🔵 Dry Waste

  • Plastic bottles
  • Paper, cardboard
  • Glass, metal cans

🔴 Hazardous Waste

  • Batteries
  • Medical waste
  • Chemicals

❌ Do NOT Mix

  • Wet waste with plastic
  • Batteries with dry waste
  • Sanitary waste with others
